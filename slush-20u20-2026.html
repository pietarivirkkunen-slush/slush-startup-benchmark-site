Slush reveals the first Slush 20 Under 20: twenty extraordinary supertalents from over a dozen countries who are already building rockets, robots, satellites and AI companies backed by the world’s leading investors. Between them, the twenty have raised over €10 million in venture funding, been accepted into Y Combinator, worked at Lovable, OpenAI and NASA, and booked a satellite launch for 2027. Most of them did it before finishing high school.

Slush 20U20 is Slush’s new program for the most exceptional outliers of this generation. Founders are prioritized, alongside young builders with clear entrepreneurial ambitions. The youngest European ever accepted into Y Combinator, the youngest EWOR fellow and the youngest Thiel Fellow are coming to Helsinki.

The cohort

Lovable alumni, NASA prodigies and 15-year-old supertalents

The Lovable mafia has extended to under twenties. Two of the twenty have already passed through Europe’s fastest-growing startup. Elliot Norrevik (17, Sweden) joined Lovable at 16 before becoming the youngest European ever accepted into Y Combinator. Elliot Lindberg (19, Sweden / Åland) built software with 450k+ users at 14, joined Lovable at 17, and is now building an agentic video editor.

Two of them are quite literally shooting for the stars. Devesh Khilnani (18, US) joined NASA as one of the youngest engineers working on Artemis III before leaving at 17 to build Xyla. Minh Nguyen (17, US) turned his research with NASA’s OSDR Analysis Working Group into xOrbita, a satellite autopilot whose first launch is booked for 2027.

The best are backed by the best. Arlan Rakhmetzhanov (19, Kazakhstan) has raised €6.2 million from the likes of CRV, LocalGlobe and BoxGroup for his YC-backed consumer AI startup Nozomio. Nick Dobroshinsky (16, US) became the youngest Thiel Fellow to date after growing his AI equity research platform EveryTicker to 60,000 monthly users.

The world’s best talent is only getting younger. At 15, Kaon Krasniqi (Kosovo) and Amitav Krishna (Canada) have already achieved more than most people double their age. Kaon ran an edtech startup before organising Kosovo’s biggest AI hackathon. Amitav joined the €100 million+ scaleup 8090 Solutions before presenting his quantum research at IEEE’s qCCL 2026 conference.

Slush 20U20 2026

The full list, from youngest to oldest

Amitav Krishna

15 · Canada

Breakthrough research on quantum and water splitting

Amitav has done research on quantum denoising and is currently researching materials simulations for water splitting. He has presented his findings at IEEE’s qCCL 2026 conference, and joined the $100M+ scaleup 8090 Solutions at 14.

Kaon Krasniqi

15 · Kosovo

Founder of Kosovo’s biggest AI hackathon

Kaon started coding at seven and was running an edtech startup at 14. He organized Kosovo’s biggest AI hackathon with over 100 participants, and is now focusing on building AR glasses. Earlier this year, Kaon became the youngest member of Null Fellows, a program connecting the greatest entrepreneurial minds of the next decades.

Nick Dobroshinsky

16 · United States

Youngest Thiel Fellow

Nick is the founder of EveryTicker, an AI-powered equity research platform used by 60,000 investors monthly. He started it when he was fourteen and is now extending the platform into asset management by launching a hedge fund that uses EveryTicker to automate investment in public equities. He became the youngest Thiel Fellow to date at 16.

David K.

17 · Sweden

Ex-Strawberry, now building energy-based models

David dropped out of high school to work at Strawberry, one of the world’s leading AI browser startups. From Strawberry he went on to found Polished Snow, a startup building energy-based models.

Edvin Andersson

17 · Sweden

Serial founder with €2M+ in generated revenue

Edvin is already building his third startup. After making $2M+ in revenue with his past ventures, he recently founded Grit to transform home improvement with AI.

Elliot Norrevik

17 · Sweden

Youngest European accepted to Y Combinator (YC S26)

Elliot launched his first company at 14 and joined Lovable at 16 as a software engineer. He then co-founded Blaise and got accepted to Y Combinator, becoming the youngest European ever to do so. He has now left Blaise to research the existential impact of superintelligence.

Minh Nguyen

17 · United States

Ex-NASA and youngest EWOR fellow

Minh turned his research with NASA’s OSDR Analysis Working Group into xOrbita, an autopilot platform for satellites. He soon became the youngest EWOR fellow ever. xOrbita allows spacecraft to make collision-avoidance decisions autonomously in real time, and is backed by Pareto Holdings, Emergent Ventures and Frontera. Its first satellite launch is booked for 2027.

Devesh Khilnani

18 · United States

Ex-NASA, building containers to space

Devesh started college at 14, dropped out at 16, and joined NASA as an IC as one of the youngest engineers working on Artemis III. At 17, Devesh left NASA to build his own startup, Xyla. Xyla builds containers to produce plants in extreme environments ranging from oil rigs and mining fields to space.

Divyansh Chauhan

18 · India

Ex-Slashy (YC S25), now building a simulation dataset

Divyansh moved from India to San Francisco after high school to join Slashy (YC S25) as the first hire, and made six-figure revenue on the side with Blip AI. He is now back in India building Datak, a startup commercializing CAD and engineering simulation datasets.

Fifi Siddiqui

18 · United Kingdom

Serial founder and tech prodigy

Fifi taught herself to code at 7 and has been building businesses since 11. She dropped out at 17 and moved from London to Miami to build Maxed, an AI-native operating system for CPA firms, with $895k in pre-seed funding raised.

Maximilian Lippert

18 · Brazil / Germany

Raised from Atlantic on his 18th birthday

Maximilian has been running businesses since the age of 13. On his 18th birthday this August, Atlantic VC backed his newest venture, Flocta, with a $1.1 million deal only two weeks after starting. He is now building Europe’s first truly cost-efficient AI platform for working with worldwide AI models at significantly lower cost, making AI affordable at scale.

Noel Matero

18 · Finland

Robotics and informatics prodigy

Noel has represented Finland in the Robotics World Championships and the International Olympiads in Informatics. He finished high school this spring and spoke with more than a dozen startups interested in hiring him before deciding to join Verda to build LLM systems.

Rahul Chandelkar

18 · India

Youngest Magnificent Grants Fellow

Growing up with limited internet access in a small village in India, Rahul taught himself computer science and cybersecurity, earning his first bug bounty at 13. He later joined a world-leading Capture the Flag team and became the youngest-ever Magnificent Grants Fellow. Rahul is now building a new cybersecurity lab, with the ambition of building a DeepMind for security.

Arlan Rakhmetzhanov

19 · Kazakhstan

YC (S25) founder with €6.2M raised

Arlan dropped out of high school to build Nozomio, a consumer AI startup. Nozomio was accepted to Y Combinator in summer 2025, and has since received $6.2 million in funding from the likes of CRV, LocalGlobe and BoxGroup. Arlan previously founded an edtech startup at 15, reaching 20,000 users.

Con Kirby

19 · Ireland

Chess prodigy turned OpenAI forward deployed engineer

Con has won three all-Ireland junior chess championships, before shifting into software engineering. At 17 he won TECS, Ireland’s student software engineering competition. He later founded his first startup, Icon, and currently works at OpenAI as a forward deployed engineer.

Elliot Lindberg

19 · Sweden / Åland

Serial founder and ex-Lovable

Elliot built software with 450k+ users when he was 14. After founding numerous small businesses, he joined Lovable at 17. He is now building an agentic video editor.

Julian Lewandowski

19 · Ireland / Poland

Serial hackathon winner and founding engineer at Zymbly (YC W26)

Julian is a serial hackathon winner with nearly $50,000 in hackathon prize money. At 15, he designed an award-winning motor-free retractable flood wall for coastal cities. He is now the first hire and a founding forward-deployed engineer at Zymbly (YC W26), where he builds AI agents for aircraft maintenance crews after landing the role by cold emailing the founders.

Liam Fuller

19 · Ireland

AI founder with €1.4M raised

Liam built his first e-commerce app at 13, and raised $1.4 million for his newest startup, Source, at 17. Source is an end-to-end AI-native system integrator for legacy industries.

Sofia Lipkevych

19 · Ukraine

Robotics prodigy and hackathon founder

Sofia started researching nanocrystals in high school. She founded Ukraine’s first high-school hackathon, and moved to Finland at 18 to build Tune Robotics at FR8 hacker house. Her work then took her to MIT, where her studies and research target spatial understanding in robotics.

Stepan Feduniak

19 · Ukraine

Co-founder and CTO of InLoop Robotics (YC P26)

Stepan is the co-founder and CTO of InLoop Robotics, a German YC-backed startup building robot workers for warehouses. Before founding InLoop, he competed in Olympiad mathematics, did robotics research at two robot learning labs at 18, and won a week-long humanoid robot hackathon as a solo team.

One week, one city

Twenty supertalents, flown to Helsinki

Slush Week is the one week of the year when Europe’s best founders and investors are in the same city. On November 17–19, the twenty program participants are flown to Helsinki, accommodated together, and connected with top investors, Slush stage speakers and leading tech journalists through a curated mentoring program. Slush 2026 takes place in Helsinki on November 18–19.
